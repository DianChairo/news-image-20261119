A 1024×1024 editorial front-page infographic that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in six verified stories. A full-width lead panel covers the first trilateral summit of Trump, Xi and Putin in Shenzhen on the APEC sidelines; a two-column grid in the middle covers Iran widening its attacks on US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, the global rollout of Grand Theft Auto VI after a 13-year wait, House Democrats nominating Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker, and NASA confirming Voyager 1 is one light-day from Earth; a bottom strip closes the page with Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India's east coast. Everything is drawn in pure Pillow shapes and text — no photos, no network calls — in a deep teal, amber and cream palette.

`build_image.py` renders the infographic to `entry.png`, and `main.py` copies that file into the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
